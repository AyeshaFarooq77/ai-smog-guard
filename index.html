          How it works

          1. We read the live air quality

            The AQI for your city comes from a live data source, not from a stored list.

          2. We look at who you are

            Your age group, any breathing or heart condition, pregnancy and outdoor work change how risky the same air is.

          3. The AI writes your guidance

            You get advice on going out, masks, home steps and warning signs, and you can ask follow-up questions.

          Who it helps

          Children
          Smaller lungs and more time playing outside.
          Older adults
          Higher risk of heart and breathing problems on bad air days.
          Pregnant women
          Extra care is advised when air quality drops.
          People with asthma or heart conditions
          Smog can trigger symptoms even at moderate levels.
          Outdoor workers
          Riders, vendors and labourers cannot simply stay home.

          AI Smog Health Guide is a prototype built for the STCS AI Hackathon at Minhaj University Lahore, under SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being.

          It gives general prevention guidance only. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
